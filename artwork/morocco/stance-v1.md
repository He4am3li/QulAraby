Approved source portraits remain intact under `public/avatars/original-rigs/morocco`. Versioned inward-facing body art preserves identities, the brown/gold djellaba and red fez, and the emerald/gold kaftan and jewelry. The pair retains Yassine on the right and Kenza on the left in both interface languages. Both use the shared 824 × 1536 camera. Kenza's body has a 24px vertical registration adjustment to match Yassine's source foot plane exactly at y1511.

Nine generated expression plates per actor are registered into independent eye, eyebrow and mouth overlays. `register-moroccan-stance.cjs` crops and feathers these authored raster features without replacing the full face. FV uses the existing E approximation. Mouth movement follows the actual output-audio clock, with silence returning to the source closed mouth. Listeners never read the partner's mouth cues. Gaze changes happen beneath a blink. Reduced motion and loading use the new full-body static source.
